The Person Behind It

Hi, I'm Heiner.

I design brands, menus, signage and websites for small businesses — and I build the sites myself, rather than handing off designs for someone else to implement.

My background is in web development, so I approach every project from both directions: how it looks and how it actually works once a customer is holding their phone or standing at the counter. A logo that looks great but doesn't translate to a menu board, or a website that looks nice but loads slowly on mobile, isn't finished — it just looks finished.

I especially like working with local, independent businesses — food trucks, restaurants, small shops — where a few well-made pieces make a real, visible difference in how customers see the business. I currently take on a limited number of projects at a time, so each one gets real attention rather than being one of fifty on a list.
